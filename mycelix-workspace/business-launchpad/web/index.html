Optional 12-month cash runway scenario

A separate, editable month-by-month scenario. Sales units describe activity, not collections. Sales receipts and operating payments can be shifted to different months. Investing cash covers asset disposals and long-term asset purchases; financing cash covers loan/capital receipts and repayments or owner distributions. The other/unclassified fields preserve cash movements that have not been categorized; they affect closing cash but are excluded from operating/investing/financing subtotals until you classify the underlying transaction. This avoids guessing the meaning of old scenario data. Inputs are cash-only: non-cash transactions are not modeled, and all categories are planning aids—not accounting advice. The worksheet does not reconcile invoices, aged receivables, supplier balances or loan statements. Generated operating outflows combine base variable and fixed monthly costs; when edited, enter the total operating payment expected that month. Do not enter the same transaction in multiple buckets. Reset regenerates all 12 months and overwrites every cash-plan edit.

Terms-based collection and variable-cost payment schedule

This creates a separate comparison case; it does not overwrite your base cash plan. Sales receipts are derived from sales units × selling price. Per-unit variable costs are treated as supplier/payable costs, derived from units × variable cost, and allocated across the same three timing buckets. This simplified assumption may not fit every variable-cost type; different terms need separate categories. Fixed monthly costs and setup costs remain due in their current months. Each row must total exactly 100%. These are timing assumptions, not invoice-level records.

Not net profit or a go/no-go decision. The worksheet does not calculate taxes, debt service, owner draw, depreciation, demand, seasonality or costs you have not entered. It does not verify licenses or tell you whether you can legally operate at a specific location.
What happens to my data?

Calculations happen in the browser. The application does not upload inputs or persist them in browser storage. You can explicitly import a local JSON scenario or download the current assumptions and calculated report as files. Static page assets are still fetched from the server hosting this prototype. These files are not a hosted account, backup service or durable business record system; keep important files in your own backup process.
