Optional 12-month cash runway scenario

A separate, editable month-by-month scenario. Sales units describe activity, not collections. Sales cash receipts and operating cash payments are entered separately for each month, so you can move receipts later or payments earlier. Defaults initially mirror same-month sales/costs for convenience only. Setup costs are deducted before Month 1; the working-capital buffer is a minimum cash reserve, not another payment. Other cash inflows/outflows are added on top of the separately entered sales receipts and operating payments. The worksheet does not reconcile actual invoices, aged receivables or supplier balances. If you change the steady-state target above, Reset regenerates all 12 months from the current unit economics and overwrites every cash-plan edit. Otherwise, the values you enter here are kept.

Not net profit or a go/no-go decision. The worksheet does not calculate taxes, debt service, owner draw, depreciation, demand, seasonality or costs you have not entered. It does not verify licenses or tell you whether you can legally operate at a specific location.
What happens to my data?

Calculations happen in the browser. The application does not upload inputs or persist them in browser storage. You can explicitly import a local JSON scenario or download the current assumptions and calculated report as files. Static page assets are still fetched from the server hosting this prototype. These files are not a hosted account, backup service or durable business record system; keep important files in your own backup process.

Business Launchpad is experimental software. Verify assumptions, source documents, local rules and financial details independently.
