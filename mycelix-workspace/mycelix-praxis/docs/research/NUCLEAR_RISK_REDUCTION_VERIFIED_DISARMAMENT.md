# Nuclear Risk Reduction and Verified Disarmament Evidence Framework (NRVDEF)

**Status:** research proposal; not an inspection authority or treaty body  
**Scope:** risk reduction, evidence integrity, verification support and staged disarmament governance.

## Purpose

Investigate a verifiable, staged process for reducing nuclear risks and supporting eventual disarmament. The framework is about evidence, governance, independent verification and strategic stability. It does not design nuclear weapons, delivery systems, targeting, launch or command systems.

## Paradigm shift

Treat disarmament as a staged, verifiable, evidence-carrying process rather than a one-time political declaration.

A claim that a warhead or delivery system has been dismantled requires an agreed protocol, authorized physical verification, independent evidence, chain of custody, safeguards and dispute resolution. A digital receipt may establish provenance and integrity of a record; it cannot itself certify physical dismantlement or replace an authorized inspector.

## Workstreams

1. **Crisis risk reduction:** communication channels, incident notification, advance notification of exercises/tests and procedures for ambiguous events.
2. **Alert-risk reduction:** evaluate reciprocal policy options that reduce mistaken or accidental launch risk without assuming away legitimate security concerns.
3. **Verifiable limits:** evidence models for aggregate declarations, agreed data exchanges, inspections and dispute resolution.
4. **Dismantlement evidence:** custody-preserving records for declared milestones, while keeping sensitive design information outside shared records.
5. **Fissile-material safeguards:** auditable declarations and interfaces to authorized safeguards institutions, without exposing sensitive facility or material-accounting details.
6. **Non-proliferation:** support safeguards, monitoring and international cooperation.
7. **Strategic stability:** assess how missile defense, conventional systems, cyber operations, space capabilities and AI-enabled decision support can alter escalation incentives.
8. **Civilian assurance:** evaluate progress by reduced expected civilian harm and reduced risk of miscalculation, not by advantage to one state.

## Verification principles

- Authorized physical inspectors and agreed institutions retain authority for physical verification.
- Digital evidence supports provenance, integrity and audit trails, not physical truth.
- Sensitive information is minimized, compartmentalized and disclosed only under agreed rules.
- Independent witnesses and tamper-evident custody records are used when the verification protocol requires them.
- Discrepancies remain visible and trigger dispute resolution.
- False, incomplete and uncertain declarations are explicitly modeled.
- No software-generated compliance badge substitutes for treaty procedures or legal determinations.

## Benchmark programme

Use only synthetic and non-sensitive records to test:
- Missing, duplicated, contradictory and stale declarations.
- Broken custody chains, replay and credential compromise.
- Independent corroboration and false-accept / false-reject rates.
- Verification latency and inspection burden.
- Privacy leakage and unauthorized disclosure.
- Dispute-resolution time and unresolved-claim accounting.
- Protocol versioning, inspector authority and audit traceability.
- Robustness to incomplete participation and withdrawal.

No weapon-design, targeting, launch-sequence, facility-vulnerability or operational command data is part of this benchmark.

## Relationship to GCPN

The Global Cooperative Protection Network can carry agreed warning and incident records. NRVDEF defines evidence and governance requirements for risk-reduction commitments. Neither creates a supranational command authority, physical inspection authority or unilateral legal compliance determination.

## Proposed acceptance targets (not achieved results)

- 100% traceability of digital evidence to the declared custodian and protocol version.
- Zero silent deletion or coercion of disputed verification records.
- Explicit separation of record authenticity, measurement accuracy and legal authority.
- Independent reproduction of all synthetic evidence-integrity tests.
- Every unresolved discrepancy remains visible.
- No "verified dismantlement" status absent an authorized protocol and physical verification result.

## Non-claims

No unilateral disarmament guarantee, inspection certification, treaty compliance determination, nuclear command authority, operational military capability or SOTA superiority is claimed.
