Encrypted Mail

Real-Time Chat

Experimental interface. Real-time chat is outside the supported working-alpha claim.

📹

Video Calls

Experimental browser-media prototype. Relay, metadata, and session-authentication properties depend on deployment and are not alpha guarantees.

📅

Unified Calendar

Experimental interface. External calendar synchronization is not part of the canonical alpha.

🛡

Trust-Gated Inbox

The restricted DNA includes signed trust state and sender-policy primitives; economic staking remains roadmap work.

🔒

Revocable Email

Revocation concepts remain experimental. The current alpha does not claim that already-delivered plaintext can be made unreadable.

Research roadmap

The concepts below are product direction, not working-alpha guarantees.

🪙

Economic Email

Send payments, invoices, and bounties inside messages. Settled atomically on the DHT. No PayPal, no bank, no 3-5 business days.

🧠

Attention Budgets

Set a daily cognitive budget. Your calendar auto-declines meetings when you're depleted. The first calendar that actually protects your time.

🌐

Works Without Internet

Full offline support with CRDT sync. Edit, compose, and schedule — everything merges conflict-free when you reconnect.

🏛

Thread → Organization

When a chat thread reaches critical mass, it can formalize into a working group with its own treasury, governance, and resources. No paperwork.
