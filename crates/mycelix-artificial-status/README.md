# mycelix-artificial-status

Pure `#![no_std]` AMSAP-002 substrate for structurally validating artificial-status observations.

This crate owns only:

- `C` consciousness-relevant evidence bands;
- `V` valence/welfare evidence bands;
- `A` agency bands;
- `I` operational identity-continuity bands;
- `R` responsibility/moral-competence bands;
- the ten-dimensional AMSAP-001 `EvidenceProfile`.

It intentionally does **not** define or grant legal standing (`L`), governance authority (`G`), deployment currentness, external-effect authority, personhood, or a consciousness verdict.

`WellFormedArtificialStatusObservation` means only that the supplied observation satisfies this crate's conservative structural coherence rules. It is evidence-shaped input for later review, not authority.

High agency, persistent identity, or moral-rule competence cannot manufacture consciousness or welfare status. Positive `C`/`V` claims require explicit evidence, and the highest claim band requires broad strong support with weak/moderate—not strong—counterevidence.
