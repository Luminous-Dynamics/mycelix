# mycelix-stewardship-claims

STEW-004 is the evidence-bearing stewardship-claim theorem.

It represents a claim that a principal has a stewardship relationship to an exact representation, a revision, or a logical subject. The claim is deliberately **not** an authority grant and carries no reputation score, token weight, MATL weight, voting weight, or automatic winner logic.

## Core theorem

```text
claim recorded
!= claim verified
!= stewardship authority granted
!= cultural legitimacy established
!= access authority granted
```

A claimant may state that it acts for a collective, but that statement does not prove representation of that collective.

```text
represented_collective = X
!= X authorized claimant
```

## Explicit target breadth

The target scope is explicit:

- exact representation;
- exact revision;
- logical subject.

A broad target is therefore visible in the claim rather than silently inferred from an exact artifact.

## Evidence-bearing

Every v1 claim requires at least one opaque evidence reference. STEW-004 does not evaluate the evidence; later admission/resolution layers must do that. This allows oral/community testimony, credentials, institutional records, legal documents, lineage records, provenance objects, or other evidence to be referenced without privileging one evidence system in the structural theorem.

## Claimed domains

A claim states the stewardship domains it concerns, such as metadata curation, provenance, preservation, access-policy participation, cultural protocol, attribution, or reciprocity. These are **claimed domains**, not permissions.

## Non-claims

No creator status, ownership, copyright, cultural legitimacy, community mandate, legal title, namespace control, access right, policy authority, preservation authority, reciprocity right, or real-world consent is established by this crate.
