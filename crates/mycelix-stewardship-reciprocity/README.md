# Mycelix Stewardship Reciprocity — STEW-010

This crate represents **asserted reciprocity obligations** attached to one exact STEW-001 representation.

It deliberately separates:

```text
reciprocity obligation recorded
!= obligation legitimate
!= beneficiary authority verified
!= trigger occurred
!= benefit delivered
!= obligation satisfied
```

The v1 benefit vocabulary is intentionally broader than money. It can represent attribution, compensation, revenue sharing, return of research/data/copies, capacity building, infrastructure support, community investment, access benefits, environmental stewardship, knowledge return, and opaque extension terms.

Detailed amounts, percentages, schedules, legal terms, cultural protocols, and execution conditions live behind opaque requirement/basis references rather than being invented by this theorem.

Every obligation binds an **exact representation**. Work-wide or lineage-wide reciprocity inheritance must be an explicit later theorem.

This crate establishes no stewardship authority, cultural legitimacy, legal enforceability, payment, consent, or satisfaction state.
