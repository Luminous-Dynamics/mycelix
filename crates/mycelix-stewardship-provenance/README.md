# mycelix-stewardship-provenance

STEW-006 is the typed, evidence-bearing provenance theorem for Mycelix stewardship.

It records explicit predecessor → successor lineage between exact STEW-001 representations. Provenance is kept separate from epistemic truth, ownership, permission, and cultural legitimacy.

## Core theorem

```text
provenance edge exists
!= factual truth
!= copyright ownership
!= stewardship authority
!= permission to use
!= cultural legitimacy
```

## Direction

Each edge means:

```text
successor RELATION predecessor
```

For example, with relation `TranslatedFrom`, the successor is asserted to be translated from the predecessor.

## Evidence-bearing

Every edge requires at least one opaque evidence reference. Participants may also be listed with explicitly assertion-labelled roles. STEW-006 records those assertions but does not authenticate the agents or verify the evidence.

## No silent lineage

Translation, restoration, digitization, migration, remixing, performance, quotation, copying, annotation, and extraction are explicit provenance relations. They are not inferred from representation type or matching identifiers.

## Graph non-claims

A single edge theorem does not establish global DAG-ness, acyclicity, completeness, earliest origin, canonical lineage, or exclusive authorship. Those require later graph-level validation.

## Non-claims

No authorship, ownership, originality, permission, legal title, community consent, cultural authority, factual truth, signature validity, evidence validity, or preservation success is established.
