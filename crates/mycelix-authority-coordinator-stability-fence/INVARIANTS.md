# Authority Coordinator Stability Fence v0.1 — Normative Invariants

Status: **pure before/after deployment stability theorem; native bracketing/atomicity/effect admission remain out of scope**

## 1. A deployment match is only a snapshot

`QualifiedCoordinatorDeploymentComposition` proves exact current-release/target/observed-code equality at its pre observation. It does not prove coordinator code remains unchanged afterward.

## 2. The post observation must be strictly later

Positive fencing requires `post_observed_at_ms > pre_observed_at_ms`.

Reusing the same observation, an older observation, or reversing the interval denies.

## 3. The post observation must occur inside the pre evidence window

`post_observed_at_ms <= pre_composition.valid_until_ms` is mandatory.

A post observation obtained after the pre composition's evidence window cannot retroactively establish a bounded continuous admission interval.

## 4. Pre composition must still be live at qualification

After interval ordering checks, the pre composition must still be live at `now_ms`.

An expired pre composition cannot be revived by a newer matching observation.

## 5. The exact current release and target CellId are preserved

The fence reconstructs the required deployment from the exact current release embedded in the pre composition plus the exact pre target agent.

The reconstructed requirement digest/profile must equal the pre composition's requirement identity.

## 6. #262 performs the post exact-set match

The post observation is matched only through `match_required_coordinator_deployment`.

The fence does not implement a second coordinator-set equality algorithm. Missing, substituted, duplicate or unexpected coordinator code remains fail-closed through #262.

## 7. Post CellId must equal the pre target CellId

The post match must retain the exact DNA hash and exact cell agent public key bound by the pre composition.

## 8. Every fence is bound to one admission subject and one attempt nonce

`CoordinatorAdmissionSubject` commits:

- exact subject digest/profile;
- non-zero per-attempt nonce; and
- attempt reference.

Changing the subject or attempt nonce changes fence identity.

The subject type remains deserializable evidence/data; live subject provenance and nonce generation belong to the future native admission orchestrator.

## 9. Positive stability fence is non-deserializable

`QualifiedCoordinatorStabilityFence` derives `Serialize` but not `Deserialize`.

It can only be constructed by the local pure fence theorem.

## 10. Fence identity commits both observations and the admission attempt

The fence digest commits:

- pre composition digest/profile;
- post #262 match digest/profile;
- admission-subject binding digest/profile;
- pre and post observation timestamps; and
- final evidence window.

## 11. Fence lease is monotone

`verified_at = max(pre composition, post observation)`

`valid_until = min(pre composition, post observation)`

A second observation never widens the first composition's reusable horizon.

## 12. Stability is not atomicity

Two matching observations prove no detected coordinator change between those observations under their evidence assumptions. They do **not** prove `UpdateCoordinators` could not execute immediately after the post observation.

This crate provides no mutex, conductor transaction, generation lock or update exclusion mechanism.

## 13. Pure fence does not prove native bracketing provenance

Pure qualification does not prove:

- the pre/post observations came from #264's native observer;
- the observations actually bracketed the relevant live admission work;
- the admission nonce was generated by trusted native code; or
- the selected subject is the effect a consumer will execute.

Those causal/origin facts remain a later native orchestration theorem.

## 14. No execution/effect authority

`QualifiedCoordinatorStabilityFence` is deployment stability evidence only. It grants no lifecycle, executor, effect-safety or external-effect permission.

## 15. Provisioning remains blocked

Before effect-capable provisioning the stack still requires live target/release/currentness verifier provenance, real native pre/post observation orchestration, a policy for update races after the final observation, and final lifecycle/effect admission.
