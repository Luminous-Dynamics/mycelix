# MYC-CAP-002A1 portable-evidence nonclaims

The portable receipt is evidence, not self-authenticating PASS.

A valid bundle proves only that its bounded evidence can be replayed against the exact frozen MYC-CAP-002A subject and reproduces that subject's fixed Return Envelope qualification surface.

It does not establish:

- legal title transfer or redemption completion;
- accounting-standard or tax compliance;
- securities-law compliance or investor suitability;
- truth of external accounting/evidence references;
- project solvency, market value, or fair pricing;
- infrastructure safety, service quality, or performance;
- handback readiness or operational sovereignty;
- community-governance legitimacy;
- security assurance outside the bounded verifier/test surface;
- qualification of any newer branch head merely because it descends from the qualified subject.

Hosted run identifiers and artifact metadata are discovery/provenance data. Independent PASS requires exact-subject source commitments plus successful offline replay.
