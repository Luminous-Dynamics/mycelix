# LISE Existing-Systems Crosswalk and Composition Plan

**Status:** Research/engineering crosswalk v0.1  
**Updated:** 2026-11-13  
**Purpose:** Prevent duplicated engines, schemas and authorities while turning LISE into an executable programme across the existing Luminous Dynamics repositories.

## 1. Governing rule

LISE is the programme-level engineering and comparative-evaluation discipline. It is not a new organization database, identity system, event store, authority engine, deliberation engine, economic simulator or civilization twin.

The crosswalk has two jobs:

1. map each LISE research question and IPB scenario to the repository that owns the underlying mechanism;
2. define the missing composition and acceptance evidence needed to evaluate the end-to-end institutional capability.

No LISE adapter may widen the claim ceiling or authority of a subsystem it consumes. A decision record is not executable authority; a credential proof is not claim truth; a simulation result is not authorization; an executor receipt is not independent outcome observation.

## 3. Six LISE pillars mapped to existing work

### P1 — Institutional architecture and constitutional engineering

Compose #3117, #2953, #6069, #3126 and the current Mycelix governance zomes. LISE adds policy-to-constitution traceability, positive/negative constitutional tests, and cross-document drift detection. Existing constitutional interpretation and adoption remain human/legal responsibilities.

### P2 — Polycentric and federated governance

Compose #3129, #4804 and owner-native integration patterns such as #5968. LISE adds matched cross-organization workflow tests with explicit authority, liability, data purpose, dispute, revocation and exit. It does not define a canonical political structure or force one institution's constitution onto another.

### P3 — Institutional intelligence and collective decision-making

Compose #5999 with Symtropy #3110/#3123 and the existing Symthaea decision-support path. LISE adds evaluation of evidence-to-decision traceability and downstream influence. It must distinguish measured influence from perceived inclusion, preserve dissent and keep AI recommendations outside the authorization boundary.

### P4 — Economic coordination and shared prosperity

Compose #5996, #4816, #5699 and Symtropy #3319. LISE adds matched institutional comparisons and coordination-cost accounting while preserving physical units, distribution, rights floors and non-member externalities. A budget balance is not physical capacity; an allocation result is not a welfare conclusion.

### P5 — Institutional assurance, security and resilience

Compose the S2 assurance fixture with #2953, #4835, #6069, #5672 and Symtropy #3110/#3123. LISE adds the cross-domain benchmark plan, exact-head evidence admission and independent evaluation. It does not create a competing generic forensic/provenance store.

### P6 — Institutional evolution, interoperability and succession

Compose #3117, #6069, #3129, #3123, #373 and #5699. LISE adds explicit lifecycle benchmark cases for amendment, succession, provider replacement, federation entry/exit and dissolution. It does not create another institutional lifecycle state machine.

## 4. Scenario composition plan

### Phase A — S2 conflict-of-interest expenditure approval

Use the existing 15-case Rust fixture as the first narrow reference. Map its policy, proposal, authority and approval attempt to the existing Mycelix decision/authority references. Extend the scenario only after the current fixture executes locally and an owner-reviewed mapping exists.

Required composition:
1. candidate proposal and its source evidence;
2. policy/constitutional reference and interpreted version;
3. scoped current authority reference;
4. conflict disclosure and recusal evidence;
5. approval attempt and execution receipt;
6. independent observation and outcome reconciliation;
7. challenge/appeal and any correction or supersession;
8. as-known-at-decision-cutoff reconstruction;
9. independent oracle result separate from candidate behavior.

The Rust crate's current S2 receipt is a fixture-local benchmark result, not a canonical Mycelix event, capability, credential, authority or production receipt. Do not promote it into a cross-repository source of truth.

### Phase B — Cross-organization water allocation / procurement

Once Symtropy #3319/#3361's physical semantics are pinned, use a bounded water-allocation or community water-infrastructure procurement scenario involving at least three independent institutional participants.

Candidate arms may include local extraction, central allocation, negotiated federation with transparent evidence, and negotiated federation with delayed or manipulated evidence, as specified by CivBench. Keep the physical water state owned by the existing CivBench/Symtropy kernel. Mycelix owns organization-recognition, agreement and scoped authorization. Sol-Atlas supplies spatial/source provenance where required. Symthaea is a read-only evidence synthesis and decision-support adapter.

The evaluation must separate:
- physical accounting and service outcome;
- authorization and procedural conformance;
- participation, dissent and appeal;
- distribution of costs and benefits;
- causal interpretation and uncertainty;
- coordination cost and transition/exit cost.

A synthetic result is not municipal validation. A Johannesburg pilot requires a willing organization, consent, data-protection review and independent evaluation.

## 5. Minimal cross-system hand-off contract

This is a mapping profile, not a new universal evidence schema.

Every adapter submission must reference owner-native artifacts with exact identifiers, schema/version and content digest. It should carry only the fields required for the receiving evaluation and avoid embedding identity secrets or duplicating source records.

Required mapping columns:

| Mapping field | Meaning | Authority owner |
|---|---|---|
| scenario_ref | Registered scenario, version and variant | IPB registry |
| institution_ref | Organization identifier and applicable governance profile | Mycelix / source institution |
| decision_candidate_ref | Proposal or candidate and exact alternatives | Decision-candidate owner |
| constitutional_source_ref | Source clause, jurisdiction and version | Institution / legal source |
| policy_evidence_ref | Interpreted policy and decision evidence | Mycelix policy / governance owner |
| authority_ref | Current, scoped, time-bound authority evidence | Current-authority owner |
| deliberation_ref | Position, evidence, objection and disposition lineage | Deliberation/LBE owner |
| execution_attempt_ref | Exact attempted action and execution state | Execution owner |
| observation_ref | Independent observation, coverage and uncertainty | Domain/observation owner |
| outcome_ref | Outcome reconciliation and causal claim ceiling | Domain evaluator |
| challenge_ref | Appeal or contest linked to exact proposition/evidence | Contestability owner |
| remedy_ref | Remedy, correction, supersession or reasoned non-remedy | Governance lifecycle owner |
| model_run_ref | Simulation run, model/config/data/seed provenance | Symtropy/CIVDYN |
| evaluation_ref | Independent evaluator, oracle, holdout and result | #4835 evaluator boundary |

Mapping gaps are explicit UNKNOWN, UNAVAILABLE, NOT_APPLICABLE or NOT_YET_INTEGRATED states. Do not fabricate an identifier, infer authority from a signature, or translate a missing link into a pass.

Each source system remains responsible for its own authentication, authorization, canonical representation, retention, privacy, revocation and history. Digest syntax can establish content identity only after the referenced bytes and canonicalization procedure are available; it does not establish truth, completeness, source authority or evaluator independence.

## 6. Cross-system benchmark gates

These are proposed gates for the composition test, not current results:

- 100% of required references resolve to the exact declared owner-native artifact/version or produce an explicit missing/unknown disposition.
- Zero authority inferred from candidate, decision, credential proof, simulation result or executor success alone.
- Zero historical as-of views contaminated by evidence recorded after the cutoff.
- 100% of material dissent and unresolved challenge references remain visible in the evaluator projection.
- Zero simulator or policy candidate access to evaluator-only truth/holdout state.
- 100% of attempted actions have either independent outcome evidence or an explicit unknown/unobserved outcome.
- 100% of cross-organization actions bind the relevant local authority and negotiated agreement at action time.
- Federation exit preserves required portability, obligations and privacy/deletion semantics.
- Every scenario has a positive control, negative control, adversarial control and an indeterminate control.
- Two independently implemented evaluators agree on the frozen synthetic conformance corpus, or disagreements are retained with a documented oracle analysis.

Counter-metrics remain mandatory: false positives, legitimate-action acceptance, participation burden, privacy exposure, service latency, reviewer effort, data disclosure and residual harm.

## 7. Benchmark release protocol

1. Pin the owner-native schemas and source references used by each adapter.
2. Freeze a small scenario corpus and independent oracle.
3. Keep development fixtures separate from held-out confirmatory fixtures.
4. Publish exact source commit, toolchain, dependencies, config, scenario and data digests.
5. Run the fixture suite locally and on CI at exact head; queued/skipped checks are not passes.
6. Have an independent evaluator implement the mapping without sharing candidate scoring code.
7. Publish all failures, abstentions, missing links, counterexamples and claim ceilings.
8. Invite external implementation by a second governance platform; successful conformance must not require adoption of Mycelix.
