# FIN-ECO-006 — Creditism Protocol Family v1\n\nStatus: research architecture / external normative adapter candidate\n\n## Why this exists\n\nCreditism should not be implemented as another generalized Mycelix currency. The current 2026 Common Planet design separates Personal Credit, Community Credit, Bonus, housing stewardship, contribution recognition, marketplace/exchange behavior and real-capacity constraints. Mycelix can provide neutral evidence, identity, ledger, governance, resource and marketplace infrastructure while keeping those normative semantics in an adapter.\n\nSource model: Common Planet, Creditism Architecture, 2026: https://common-planet.org/creditism/architecture\n\n## Semantic layers\n\nPersonal Credit (PC)\nNon-debt purchasing right for an individual. Baseline issuance, verified contribution issuance and Bonus issuance are separate causes. Spending deletes PC in the Creditism economic model.\n\nCommunity Credit (CC)\nNon-transferable budget for collectively authorized capacity. Individuals may direct their share through governance, but only qualified group/project accounts spend CC. CC spending deletes the unit.\n\nBonus\nSeparate outcome-recognition stream. A verified group outcome may create Bonus Personal Credit without becoming a permanent ownership claim on the productive system.\n\nHousing Stewardship\nSeparate resource/rights system. A person receives a residence right against available dwelling capacity; housing does not become a PC balance or speculative asset.\n\nContribution Recognition\nWork observation, contribution qualification, pay-rate policy and issuance decision remain distinct subjects.\n\nReal Capacity\nLabor, material, energy, water, space, inventory, repair and ecological constraints remain separate evidence domains. Monetary authorization may not manufacture capacity.\n\n## Mycelix non-equivalence table\n\nCreditism PC/CC/Bonus != MYCEL\nCreditism PC/CC/Bonus != SAP\nCreditism PC/CC/Bonus != TEND\nCreditism contribution evidence != contribution issuance\nCreditism issuance != productive capacity\nCreditism spending deletion != seller payment\nHousing stewardship != financial ownership\nCommunity Credit authorization != physical action authority\nPrice signal != wage determination\nPrice signal != capital ownership\n\n## Proposed ledger/event model\n\nUse append-only events and frontier-derived balances.\n\nPC event families:\n- BaselineIssued\n- ContributionCreditIssued\n- BonusCreditIssued\n- PersonalSpendDeleted\n- CorrectionSuperseded\n\nCC event families:\n- CommunityAllocationIssued\n- CommunityDirected\n- ProjectCapacityReserved\n- CommunitySpendDeleted\n- AllocationSuperseded\n\nEvery authority-bearing event should bind:\n\nsource subject + policy generation + authority identity + evidence references + exact target account + causal parent(s) + currentness/freshness + idempotency key\n\nHistorical evidence remains non-authorizing after supersession unless an explicit historical-reading policy requests it.\n\n## Real-resource circuit\n\nProject proposal\n-> evidence / need assessment\n-> democratic or policy authorization\n-> capacity envelope\n-> real-resource reservation\n-> staged execution\n-> observed outcome\n-> continuation / revision / termination\n\nCredit cannot skip the capacity envelope.\n\n## Capital formation\n\nThe main unresolved Creditism question is not how to delete money. It is how to coordinate long-lived capital formation without recreating debt or hidden ownership claims.\n\nCandidate mechanism:\n\nProject\n+ Community Credit capacity budget\n+ staged resource reservations\n+ milestone gates\n+ maintenance liability\n+ outcome evidence\n-> continued stewardship\n\nA failed project should release remaining unspent authorization and preserve its losses/evidence rather than create an indefinite claim against future residents.\n\n## Stability and calibration\n\nCredit stock must be observed as:\n\nCreditOutstanding(t+1) = CreditOutstanding(t) + Issuance(t) - Deletion(t)\n\nBut monetary quantity is not treated as a direct price-level oracle.\n\nAlso observe:\n- real productive capacity\n- inventories\n- utilization\n- scarcity\n- supply bottlenecks\n- ecological floors\n- credit velocity\n- import/export constraints\n- repair and replacement capacity\n\nThe current Creditism architecture explicitly states that issuance must be calibrated against real capacity and lists persistent misallocation and verification failure as failure modes. That is the correct target for deterministic stress testing.\n\n## Verification architecture\n\nThe Evidence Fabric boundary developed in Symthaea should be reused:\n\nparticipant assertion\n-> evidence object\n-> cryptographic validity\n-> exact subject/attribute/generation/context binding\n-> verifier-owned reference provenance/currentness\n-> authority/fallback semantics\n-> issuance or allocation decision\n\nA valid signature must never become authority by itself.\n\nContribution recognition should additionally require protection against surveillance, Goodhart gaming, evaluator capture, stale credentials and double attribution.\n\n## Marketplace semantics\n\nMaintain a distinction between:\n\nchoice\nprice signal\nissuance\npayment/deletion\nproducer recognition\nownership/stewardship\nphysical fulfillment\n\nThat prevents the marketplace from becoming a hidden wage, debt or capital-accumulation layer.\n\n## Failure campaign\n\nPriority adversarial scenarios:\n\n1. issuance exceeds available productive capacity;\n2. false contribution generates PC;\n3. duplicate evidence generates PC twice;\n4. stale policy generation continues issuing;\n5. participant changes their own qualification inputs;\n6. PC transfer creates an undeclared secondary market;\n7. CC is diverted into personal purchasing power;\n8. project receives CC without reserved capacity;\n9. capacity reservation is double-spent across projects;\n10. failed projects continue receiving grants;\n11. housing scarcity becomes a purchasing-power auction;\n12. resale creates covert capital appreciation;\n13. external-currency conversion becomes an extraction channel;\n14. community governance captures the issuance authority;\n15. ecological floors are overridden by economic authorization;\n16. valid attestation is bound to the wrong subject/generation/context;\n17. verifier reference is stale or participant-controlled;\n18. network partition causes duplicate issuance;\n19. frontier rollback resurrects deleted credit;\n20. governance upgrade silently changes historical accounting semantics.\n\n## Recommended Mycelix decomposition\n\nDo not create one Creditism monolith.\n\nFinance owns neutral ledger/accounting/replay machinery.\nMarketplace owns listing/offer/fulfillment mechanics.\nCommons owns shared-resource and stewardship state.\nIdentity owns participant identity and credential lifecycle.\nEvidence/Knowledge owns provenance and epistemic references.\nGovernance owns decision and authorization semantics.\nCreditism adapter owns the normative rules for PC/CC/Bonus/Stewardship and their interaction.\nSymthaea owns simulation, comparison and recommendation; it does not become monetary authority by intelligence or confidence.\n\n## Transition path\n\nCapitalist transition\n-> coexistence with ordinary money\n-> bounded Creditism pilot with explicit external-currency bridge\n-> verified contribution and Community Credit experiments\n-> capacity-calibrated project funding\n-> housing/resource stewardship pilot\n-> cross-community clearing\n-> wider adoption only after empirical field evidence\n\n## First experiment\n\nStart with a synthetic 100-person / 20-project economy.\n\nVary:\n- baseline issuance;\n- contribution issuance;\n- deletion rate;\n- real capacity;\n- verification error;\n- governance concentration;\n- market price signals;\n- external trade;\n- project duration;\n- maintenance burden;\n- shock severity.\n\nMeasure:\n- unmet essential demand;\n- idle capacity;\n- resource bottlenecks;\n- concentration of purchasing power;\n- concentration of decision authority;\n- fraudulent issuance;\n- duplicate issuance;\n- project failure externalization;\n- ecological overshoot;\n- resilience under shocks.\n\nDo not optimize a single welfare scalar in the first experiment. Preserve the vector of outcomes and expose trade-offs.\n\n## Claim ceiling\n\nThis document is a research architecture and adapter proposal. It does not establish that Creditism is stable, inflation-safe, fair, incentive-compatible, ecologically sufficient, politically robust, or superior to existing monetary systems. The next step is adversarial deterministic simulation followed by empirical field trials with explicit endpoints.
