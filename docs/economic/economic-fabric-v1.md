# Economic Fabric V1

Mycelix should be an **economic interoperability fabric**, not a mandatory currency.

The design goal is to let economically different systems exchange verified economic meaning without forcing them to share the same monetary ontology.

## Common semantic kernel

`Observation → Evidence → EconomicEvent → Policy/Valuation → Obligation/Entitlement → Offer/Allocation → Authorization → Settlement → Outcome → Correction`

The kernel deliberately separates physical facts, economic interpretation, policy, authority, and settlement.

## Integral compatibility

Integral's public Development Guide describes ITC as contribution accounting rather than money: credits are tied to recognized necessary work and are not transferable/tradable. COS records provide inputs to ITC calculations. citeturn0search12

Therefore the Integral adapter should preserve **ITC semantics as foreign/source-owned semantics**, rather than converting ITCs into a generic Mycelix currency.

## Broader interoperability

Holochain's public ecosystem already exposes hREA/Valueflows as an economic coordination approach for resources, events and agents, including supply chains, bookkeeping, ERP and alternative currencies. citeturn0search0turn0search8

Mycelix should therefore be able to interoperate with REA/Valueflows-style systems without making Valueflows the mandatory Mycelix ontology either.

## Economic OS/fabric criterion

Mycelix becomes an economic fabric when an independent system can:

1. publish an economic event;
2. preserve its original semantics and provenance;
3. have another system recognize it without rewriting its origin;
4. apply local valuation/policy;
5. create an explicit obligation/entitlement;
6. authorize a settlement;
7. settle exactly once;
8. reconcile disputes/corrections without rewriting history.

This is a stronger and more testable criterion than “supports multiple currencies.”

## Nonclaims

This contract does not establish financial performance, economic fairness, legal/regulatory status, currency legitimacy, Integral adoption, Holochain adoption, or real-world viability.
