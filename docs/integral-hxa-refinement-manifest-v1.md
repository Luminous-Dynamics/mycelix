# Integral HXA Refinement Manifest v1

This manifest makes the Human Experience Assurance boundary traceable to its
executable reference witnesses without overstating what those witnesses prove.

## Assurance state

- Status: BoundedExecutableWitness
- Source status: CandidateInterface
- Interface profile: INTEGRAL-HXA-IF01-COMPOSITION-CANDIDATE

The manifest is intentionally open to stronger assurance. Reference-model
conformance is only one stage in the path toward production refinement and
participant evidence.

## Machine-checkable scope

The HXA registry covers HXA-I01 through HXA-I12. The cross-layer composition
registry additionally covers the HXA/IF01 composition entry points.

A consistency test compares the manifest IDs, witness names, and claim ceilings
against the executable Rust registry. This is a traceability check, not a proof
that the prose, Rust semantics, and production implementation are equivalent.

## Claim ceiling

This manifest does not establish participant comprehension, appropriate
real-world reliance, override accuracy in real use, accessibility effectiveness,
satisfaction, happiness, flourishing, social legitimacy, or production correctness.

Those require their own evidence.

## Required participant evidence

The machine boundary therefore hands off to a behavioral evidence program
covering comprehension, appropriate reliance, override accuracy, uncertainty
recognition, authority recognition, AI-independent contestability, safe
degradation, accessibility/localization, and longitudinal outcomes.

The target remains appropriate reliance, not maximum trust. A participant who
correctly rejects a bad recommendation is evidence of preserved agency; a
participant who blindly accepts a correct recommendation is not, by itself,
evidence of understanding. Both correctness and the reasoning/verification
behavior around the recommendation matter.

## Non-closure rule

No scalar trust, satisfaction, happiness, flourishing, or legitimacy score may
close the manifest. The assurance path is staged:

reference conformance -> production refinement -> behavioral evidence ->
resilience -> longitudinal evidence

A stronger stage does not retroactively turn a weaker artifact into a proof of
a broader claim.
