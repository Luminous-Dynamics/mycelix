# SIF v0.1 Privacy Boundary Checklist

The accountability layer must not become a second surveillance dataset.

- pairwise subject handles instead of universal identifiers;
- notification routing stored separately from receipts;
- commitment-only cross-provider verification context;
- raw case/query/authority plaintext kept out of Xenia/Symthaea interoperability surfaces unless a narrowly scoped adapter genuinely requires it;
- minimum-disclosure result commitments;
- public transparency based on aggregates/commitments rather than person-linked receipts;
- bounded query/privacy budgets to resist mosaic reconstruction;
- mandatory notice or expiring delayed notice—no permanent-secret state;
- delayed-notice approvals and future evidence witnesses require independently verifiable authority roots, not caller-provided labels alone;
- contest/correction history should append rather than silently rewrite evidence.
